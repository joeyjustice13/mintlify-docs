title: "Tasks"
description: "What they are, how they work, and why some are locked."
---

A **task** is a one-time structured interview. You answer questions, the AI asks follow-ups, and at the end it generates a finished document that lives in your library forever.

Think of tasks as the *foundation layer*. They produce the source-of-truth documents that everything else (projects, content tracker, calendar) reads from.

## How a task runs

1. **Click the task** on your home page or in the sidebar.
2. **Have the conversation.** The AI runs an interview tailored to that task's goal. Answer honestly. Skim answers = skim document.
3. **Research phase (sometimes).** Some tasks fire off background research while you're talking. You'll see chunks streaming in.
4. **Document generation.** When you're done, the app spits out a polished document.
5. **Done.** The document is saved. The task is marked complete. You can re-read it any time.

## Prerequisites

Some tasks are locked behind others. If task B says *"Complete Brand Story first"*, that's because B can't do its job without the document A produces. It's not gatekeeping for the sake of it. The AI literally needs the upstream answer to do downstream work well.

The home page shows a lock icon on tasks that aren't ready yet. Finish the prereqs and they'll unlock automatically.

## Can I redo a task?

Yes. Open the task and hit **Restart**. Heads up: this nukes the existing document for that task. Anything downstream that referenced it will use the new version going forward.

If you just want to *tweak* something rather than redo the whole interview, you can edit the generated document directly in your **Library**.

## Where the document goes

Two places:

- **Library tab**: your full document library. Searchable. Groupable. Downloadable.
- **Auto-injected into projects**: when a project lists a task as a prerequisite, that task's generated document is fed to the AI as context every time you chat. You don't have to attach it manually. It just shows up.

## Files inside a task

Some tasks let you upload files (PDFs, docs, etc.) before or during the interview. Those files become context for that specific task only. They don't bleed into other tasks or projects unless those also reference them.
