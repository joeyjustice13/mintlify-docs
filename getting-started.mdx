Okay so here's the move. On your first login, four things happen, in this order:

## 1. Change your password

We send you an admin-created password. The first time you sign in, the app forces you to set your own. Pick something you'll remember.

## 2. Tell us about you (onboarding)

Right after the password change, a single form asks for:

- **First name** and **last name** (we use both as your display name in the sidebar)
- **Instagram handle** (just the handle, the full URL, or `@handle` all work)
- **Timezone** (auto-detected from your browser, you can override it)
- **Niche** (one-liner about who you serve, e.g. "fitness coaches")
- **Offer** (a couple sentences about what you sell and to whom)

This is a one-time thing. You can edit any of it later by clicking your name in the sidebar profile menu.

Why we ask: the Instagram handle is how we pull your post metrics into the **Metrics** tab. The niche and offer get baked into AI prompts so the assistants stop asking you to repeat yourself.

## 3. Welcome video

A short Vimeo walkthrough auto-plays once you're in. Watch it or don't, it's saved if you want it later under **Profile → Introduction Video**.

## 4. Pick your archetype

After the video, you'll be asked to choose an **archetype**: Explorer, Sovereign, or Cartographer. This isn't a vibes quiz. It tells the app what mix of content you want to make (growth content vs. authority content vs. personal content) and which content pillars to lean on.

You can change this any time under **Profile → Select Archetype**. Changing it doesn't nuke anything you've already made; it just changes what the app suggests going forward.

## 5. Look at your task list

The home page shows your tasks. They run in a specific order, and some are **locked** until you finish earlier ones. That's on purpose. Each task builds on the last.

Hit the first unlocked task. Answer the questions. When you're done, the app generates a document and you move on.

## How long this takes

The first task takes ~10 to 20 minutes if you actually think about your answers. Don't rush. The whole point of the tasks is that they pry good answers out of you, the kind you wouldn't have written in a blank doc.

If you have to stop partway through, you can. Your progress saves automatically. Just come back later and pick up where you left off.

## What you'll have when you're done

After working through the tasks:

- A document library full of your own positioning, brand story, voice, offers, etc.
- Project chats (writing assistants, strategists, whatever) that already know all of that about you.
- A content tracker pre-loaded with a calendar that matches your archetype.
- Instagram metrics quietly syncing in the background every day.

That's it. That's the on-ramp.

## Editing your profile later

Click your name (bottom left in the sidebar) → click the chip at the top of the menu that pops up. You get the same form you filled out during onboarding, pre-loaded with your current values. Update whatever, hit Save.

If you ever change your Instagram handle here, the metrics tab will pick it up on the next sync (or hit Refresh on the metrics page to sync immediately).
