# FAQ

## Do I need WorldBuilder after the program?

Yes if you still want the workspace. Your documents, Content Tracker, Studio room, chat history, and Recordings live in the app. The live cohort and coaching calls are one input. The brand system (tasks → docs → Studio / projects → tracker) is what you keep using after the live program ends.

If your subscription or access ends, export or download anything you need before then (Library documents, tracker scripts). Confirm current access terms with your coach or support. Product docs do not invent billing policy.

## Where do my setup documents go?

The three setup tasks (North Star, Citizen, WorldCode) generate documents in your Library. Once those are done and you've mapped [Your First 5 Videos](/tasks/first-five-videos), Studio unlocks and adds those docs to My Business in your room so chat has them as context.

## Recordings vs Heartbeat

Coaching call recordings with summaries and chat live in the app under Recordings. You can sort by category, watch the video, read the summary, and ask questions grounded in the transcript.

## Scripts that sound like me

Use a guided **Reel** in the Studio (brain dump → framework → structure → voice pass) instead of one-shot AI scripts. Save the final spoken draft to Content Tracker when it is ready to film.
