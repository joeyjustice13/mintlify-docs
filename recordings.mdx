**Recordings** is the shared library of coaching calls. Full replay, a Fathom-style written summary, and a chat that already has the transcript loaded. You do not have to scrub a two-hour Zoom looking for the one hot seat that actually mattered.

You'll find it in the sidebar near **Library**, labeled **Recordings**. Same library for everyone. No private folders, no "did I get invited to this one."

## What's on a recording

Open any card and you get three layers:

1. **The video**: YouTube embed, play it like a normal human
2. **The summary**: key takeaways, topics, hot seat highlights, the stuff you'd highlight if you took notes (you didn't)
3. **Chat with transcript**: ask questions against the full call text, not a vibes summary of a vibes summary

The call date sits under the title. Titles are series names only (Money Mondays, Content & Branding, etc.), so you're not decoding "Money Mondays - July 13, 2026" like it's a filing system from 2009.

## Browse + filter

The list is newest-first. Use the category menu to filter:

- Money Mondays
- Content & Branding
- Marketing
- Sales Call Feedback
- Guest Masterclasses
- Other

Click a card. Watch, skim, or open chat. Or all three, in whatever order your brain wants. There is no wrong order. There is only the order that gets you the answer.

## Chat with the transcript

Hit **Chat with transcript** on the detail page.

- **Desktop:** the chat docks as a side column next to the video and summary. No modal, no scrim, no "did I lose my place."
- **Phone / narrow:** it opens as a full-width sheet so you're not typing into a postage stamp.

Ask things like:

- "What did they say about pricing objections?"
- "Summarize the hot seat with [name]"
- "Give me the three action items from the marketing section"

The model has the full transcript as context. It is not guessing from the summary alone. Close the chat anytime; your thread for that recording sticks around, so opening it again resumes where you left off instead of amnesia-maxxing you.

## When to use this vs everything else

- Missed a live call → Recordings
- Need a clip of advice for a script → watch, then dump the takeaway into [Studio](/studio) or a [Content Tracker](/content-tracker) card
- Want "what should I post based on MY Instagram" → that's [Weekly Reviews](/weekly-reviews), different tool
- Studying someone else's breakout reel → [Radar](/radar)

Recordings is the cohort brain on tape. Your metrics tools are about *your* account. Both matter. Confusing them is how you remix a guest masterclass into a caption about your own posting cadence. Don't do that.

## Caveats (the autistic fine print)

- Only **published** calls show up here. Drafts live on the admin side until they're ready.
- If a recording has no summary yet, you'll still get the video (and chat if the transcript is attached). Empty summary ≠ broken page.
- Chat needs a transcript on the recording. No transcript, no chat brain. The button still opens the panel, but there's nothing useful to interrogate until the transcript lands.
- These are shared coaching calls, not your private 1:1s. Don't put client secrets in the chat expecting a vault. It's a study tool.

Missed the live session? Cool. The recording is the point. Go find the hot seat that was actually about your exact problem and make the AI do the scrubbing.
