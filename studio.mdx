**Studio** is the canvas workspace for building reel scripts with context sitting right next to the chat. You'll find it in the sidebar under **Create → Studio**.

<Note>
Studio is currently rolling out to admins first. If you don't see it in the sidebar yet, that's why. It's coming for everyone soon.
</Note>

## First-time setup (fill the board)

After profile onboarding you land on **Studio setup** (`/studio/setup`). Existing admins with an empty board also get a dismissible **Build your Studio board** banner. Either path runs the same progress page:

1. Seeds **My Business** from your niche and offer
2. Syncs Instagram and imports up to **3 of your top video outliers** into **My Voice + Content** (as voice samples)
3. Optionally watches 2–3 inspiration handles on [Radar](/radar) and imports **1 top video** each into **Inspiration**

Failed steps show the error inline with **Retry**. You can always **Open Studio** anyway. That kicks off a fresh board-aware chat and the board tour so you're not dumped into an old empty-board conversation. Re-run anytime via **Build my board** in the zoom controls.

Setup is the *data* step. It does not replace the board tour.

## Board tour (how to use it)

The first time you open Studio, a short coach-mark tour walks the five sections and the chat. Replay it anytime with **Tour**. If setup already filled some cards, the tour still makes sense. It just points at what's already there. Empty board? Start with **Build my board**, then come back for the tour.

## What it is

Five sections on a board (business, your voice + content, expert brain, inspiration, video ideas), plus an interactive chat node that can see everything you've pinned. Each section's **Add** menu is tailored (e.g. voice = reel + voice note; inspiration = reel + link). The chat uses that board as context, so it isn't starting from a blank prompt every time. A compact 5-step checklist above the chat chips shows which sections are filled.

## Why it helps with scripts

- Quick chips above the chat: **5 hooks**, **Reel script** (enabled once voice or ideas has content), **Carousel outline**, **Develop my ideas**
- Import your own reels into **My Voice + Content** and they feed the same voice-sample pool [Write for me](/content-tracker#write-for-me-the-ghostwriter) uses on the tracker
- Hover an AI reply and **Save to tracker** extracts a clean **Scripted** card into your [Content Tracker](/content-tracker)

If you just need to knock out this week's three scripts, the [Content Tracker](/content-tracker) **Write this week** / **Draft all** flow is still the shortest path. Studio is for when you want the research and inspiration laid out while you write.
