Okay so here's the move. Your first login is five steps, in this order. We made it linear on purpose. Trust the process. We are NOT building a brand by clicking around vibes-first.

## 1. Set your password

Check your email for an invite from us. Click **Set your password** and pick something you'll remember (or use a password manager like a functional adult, your call). The link works for 7 days. If it expires, the page lets you send yourself a fresh one.

Forgot your password later? Hit **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and we'll email you a link that works for 1 hour.

Your account might include only part of the app, like just the course or just your documents. If so, you'll only see those parts, and some of the steps below won't apply to you.

## 2. Tell us about you (the onboarding form)

Right after you set your password, a single form asks for:

- **First name** + **last name** (both used as your display name in the sidebar)
- **Instagram handle** (the bare handle, the full URL, or `@handle`, we parse all three)
- **Timezone** (auto-detected from your browser, override if it guessed wrong)
- **Niche** (one-liner about who you serve, e.g. "fitness coaches" or "B2B founders")
- **Offer** (couple sentences about what you sell + who you sell it to)
- **Weekly posting goal** (how many posts a week you're committing to. It starts at 3, and you can change it whenever)

This is one-and-done. You can edit any of it later by clicking your name in the sidebar profile menu, so don't agonize.

**Why we ask:** the IG handle is how we pull your Instagram data into your dashboard and the **Posts** tab. The niche and offer let the AI assistants stop asking you to repeat yourself every five minutes. The timezone makes "yesterday" mean yesterday. The posting goal is what your home page and your [Monday week setup](/weekly-reviews#monday-week-setup) score you against.

After you hit save, you land on your **Home** page, where the setup checklist shows your next step. Your Instagram posts sync in the background, so your numbers fill in on their own. Once the [Studio](/studio) opens, **Build your room** pulls your business info and top reels into it in about two minutes.

## 3. Welcome video

A short Vimeo walkthrough auto-plays once you're in. Watch it, skip it, whatever. It lives forever at **Profile → Introduction video** if you want it later.

## 4. Pick your archetype

Nothing pops up to make you do this one. Click your name in the sidebar, hit **Select archetype**, and choose: Explorer, Sovereign, or Cartographer. This isn't a vibes quiz. It tells the app what mix of content you want to make (growth vs. authority vs. personal) and which pillars to lean on. It also unlocks **Generate schedule** on the tracker.

You can change it any time from the same menu. Switching doesn't nuke existing content, it just changes future suggestions. So if you're commit-phobic, just pick the closest and move on. Future-you can deal with future-you's problems.

## 5. Work the setup checklist

Your home page opens with a **"Set up your world"** checklist. It's your tasks, in order. The current one is expanded with a description and a big Start button; the rest sit collapsed below it. Some are **locked** until you finish earlier ones, and yes, that's on purpose. Each task builds on the last.

Hit Start on the expanded one. Answer the questions. When you're done, the app spits out a document, the next task unlocks, and the checklist ticks forward. Finish all of them and the checklist disappears, leaving your full dashboard: your Instagram figures, **Today's Move** (it watches your data and suggests the highest-leverage thing to do next, including opening the exact card that still needs a script), your follower trend, your best posts, and your week's content plan. That's the payoff for doing the homework.

## How long this actually takes

The first task is ~10 to 20 minutes if you actually think about your answers. Don't speedrun it. The whole reason tasks exist is that they pry good answers out of you, the kind you wouldn't have produced in a blank Notion doc at 2 a.m. while hyperfocused. That's the point. Let it cook.

If you have to stop partway, you can. Progress saves automatically. Come back, pick up where you left off, your work is fine. No tab anxiety.

## What you'll have at the end

After working through the tasks:

- A document library full of your own positioning, brand story, voice, offers, etc.
- The [Studio](/studio), your AI writers' room, which already knows all of the above about you. It's grayed out in the sidebar until your three setup interviews are done and [your first five videos](/tasks/first-five-videos) are mapped, then it lights up
- A content tracker that can lay out a calendar matching your archetype
- A home dashboard that answers "how's it going" and "what should I do next" every time you log in (followers, your posting goal, your best posts, this week's plan)
- Instagram data quietly syncing in the background, every day, forever (or until you delete your account)
- A weekly memo (the Weekly Review) landing every Monday morning that tells you what's actually working, no spreadsheet required

That's the whole on-ramp. Go answer the first task. The brand will not build itself.

## Editing your profile later

Click your name (bottom-left chip in the sidebar). The profile menu pops up. Click the chip at the TOP of that menu (the one with your avatar and "Edit profile" hint). Same form you filled out during onboarding, pre-loaded with current values. Edit, hit Save, done.

If you change your Instagram handle here, the app picks it up on the next daily sync. In a hurry? Hit Refresh on the **Posts** page (click your account chip on the dashboard to get there) and it'll sync immediately.
