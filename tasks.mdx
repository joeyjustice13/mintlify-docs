A **task** is a one-time structured interview. You answer questions, the AI asks follow-ups, and at the end it generates a finished document that lives in your library forever (or until you delete it, but rude).

Think of tasks as the *foundation layer*. They produce the source-of-truth documents that everything else (projects, content tracker, calendar, weekly reviews) reads from. So when a project chat already knows your brand voice, that's not vibes, that's a task you finished three weeks ago doing its job in the background.

## How a task runs

1. **Click the task** on your home page. While you still have tasks left, a little **Setup** counter chip sits next to Home in the sidebar keeping score.
2. **Have the conversation.** The AI runs an interview tailored to that task's goal. Answer honestly. Skim answers = skim document, that's just how it is.
3. **Research phase (sometimes).** Some tasks fire off background research while you're talking. You'll see chunks streaming in. Don't panic, it's working.
4. **Document generation.** When you're done, the app produces a polished document.
5. **Done.** The document is saved. The task is marked complete. You can re-read it any time, edit it later, the works.

## Prerequisites

Some tasks are locked behind others. If task B says *"Complete Brand Story first"*, that's because B literally cannot do its job without the document A produces. We're not gatekeeping for the bit. The AI needs the upstream answer to do downstream work well, full stop. Trust the locks. They're for you.

The home page shows a lock icon on tasks that aren't ready yet. Finish the prereqs and they unlock automatically. No refresh needed.

## Can I redo a task?

Yes. Open the task and hit **Restart**. Heads up: this nukes the existing document for that task. Anything downstream that referenced it will use the new version going forward.

If you just want to *tweak* something rather than redo the whole interview, edit the generated document directly in your **Library**. Same outcome, way less effort.

## Where the document goes

Two places, simultaneously:

- **Library tab**: your full document library. Searchable. Groupable. Downloadable.
- **Auto-injected into projects**: when a project lists a task as a prerequisite, that task's generated document gets fed to the AI as context every time you chat in that project. You don't have to attach it manually. It just shows up. That's the magic part.

## Files inside a task

Some tasks let you upload files (PDFs, docs, etc.) before or during the interview. Those files become context for that specific task only. They don't bleed into other tasks or projects unless those also reference them. Strict containment, on purpose. Your data stays where you put it.
