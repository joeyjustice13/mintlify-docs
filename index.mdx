Hi. You're in. Which means you have access to the WorldBranding app, and that part already slaps.

This is the manual. Read what's relevant, skip what isn't. The whole point of this thing is that you can build a brand without having to memorize every little knob and dial, so I wrote the docs accordingly. No corporate fluff. No "robust solutions for forward-thinking creators." Just what it does and how to use it.

## wat dis (the 30-second version)

You answer interviews, the AI builds you documents. The documents power a writers' room that knows who you are. You plan content on a kanban board. We pull your Instagram data daily so you can stop pretending you don't check your analytics. Every Monday you get a weekly memo on what's working. That's the whole app.

## What the app actually is

Three things, basically:

- **Tasks**: short, structured interviews where you answer questions and the app builds you a finished document at the end (your positioning, your offer, your story, whatever the task is for). One-and-done. The document lives in your library forever, and every other part of the app reads it automatically.
- **The [Studio](/studio)**: your AI writers' room, already stocked with your documents, your voice, and what's working for you. Bring an idea, leave with a script you can film. Unlimited conversations, and no more briefing the AI from scratch like it's your 47th day on the job.
- **Tools**: [Ideas](/ideas) (catch the thought before it evaporates), the [content tracker](/content-tracker) (plan it, write it, ship it), the [Course](/course) and [Recordings](/recordings) (lessons and coaching call replays you can chat with), your home dashboard (IG numbers, daily-refreshed), [Posts](/metrics) under Insights, [Weekly Reviews](/weekly-reviews) (AI memo on what's working, every Monday).

Tasks build your foundation. The Studio does the work on top of it. The tools tell you if any of it is landing. That's the whole model.
