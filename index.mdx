## What the app actually is

Two things, basically:

- **Tasks**: short, structured interviews where you answer questions and the app builds you a finished document at the end (your positioning, your offer, your story, whatever the task is for). One-and-done. The document lives in your library forever, and other parts of the app will use it as context automatically.
- **Projects**: open-ended AI chat assistants, each pre-loaded with a specific job (writing posts, brainstorming hooks, planning a launch, etc.). Unlimited conversations. Start a fresh one any time.

Tasks build your foundation. Projects do work on top of it. Metrics tells you if any of it is working. That's the whole model.
