Your First 5 Videos is the step right after your foundation documents, and it's the one that gets you on camera. Not scripted, not edited, not perfect. Just you, a phone, and something you actually believe.

It's also the key to the Studio. Finish your documents, map these five, and the Studio opens.

## What a yap is

Yapping is talking to camera the way you'd talk to a person. Raw, direct, opinionated, up to three minutes. It's not a polished reel, a tutorial, or a rant with no point.

The goal is reps. Five videos out, not one video perfect.

## Before you start

The first time you open Your First 5 Videos, you'll watch the **How to YAP** training. It's the framework your five videos follow, so it's worth the few minutes. Once you've watched it, hit **Yes, I watched it** and your five videos open up. Not ready yet? Hit **Not yet** and come back whenever.

Want a refresher later? **Watch the training** sits at the top of the page, anytime.

## How it works

Each video follows the same three beats:

1. **Hook**: the first few seconds. It has two jobs: stop the scroll, and make them need the rest of the video. The best hooks are for your people, a little unexpected, and easy to get on first listen.
2. **Story**: one real moment that made the hook true. What you were doing before, the exact moment it flipped, and what changed after.
3. **Value**: the one thing viewers keep. If you have three takeaways, you have three videos.

The AI walks you through them one beat at a time. It asks a question, you answer in your own words (voice notes work great here), and it pushes for specifics until the beat is real. For the hook, it offers a few options and says what each one leans on. Tap the one that sounds most like you, or write your own. When you're done with a beat, hit **Continue** to move to the next one.

## The five templates

Every video has its own template, in this order:

1. **The Contrarian** (plant your flag): *"Most coaches will tell you X. I tell my clients to do the opposite."*
2. **The Origin** (why you see it differently): *"I was doing something unexpected, and now I do this. How did it happen? Let me take you back."*
3. **The Rule I Broke** (proof through defiance): *"I stopped doing the industry norm a year ago. It's the reason I got this result."*
4. **The Heretic** (borrowed proof): *"I'm a coach and I don't believe in the thing everyone in my niche swears by."*
5. **Everything You Know Is Wrong** (your conviction, out loud): *"Everything you know about this is wrong. Take it from someone who got the result."*

The AI fills in the blanks with your stuff, pulled straight from your North Star, Citizen, and WorldCode.

## What you get

Bullets, not a script. That's on purpose. You're building the muscle of speaking freely, and a script would do the opposite.

- Each finished video shows up as a card with its **Hook**, **Story**, and **Value** bullets.
- Hit **Go deeper** on any beat when you look at a bullet and don't know what to say.
- **Save to tracker** drops it into your [Content Tracker](/content-tracker) as an idea, ready to film.
- Finish all five and you get a single document with every outline in your library.

## Tips

- **Watch the training first, for real.** The AI coaches you through the framework, but it lands way faster when you've seen it done.
- **Do all five in one sitting.** About 20 minutes total. Then film one a day.
- **Answer with the real moment.** "A client once struggled" is vague. "A client texted me at 11pm saying she'd eaten the whole sleeve of crackers again" is a story.
- **Your own words count.** Write a hook you love and it gets locked in. If something's genuinely off (like it only names the topic), the AI tells you once and offers a fix next to your line. Your pick wins either way.
- **Post before it feels ready.** It never feels ready. Score reps, not views.

## Time

About 20 minutes for all five, a few minutes per video, plus the How to YAP training the first time.

Finish your **North Star**, **Citizen**, and **WorldCode** first. Your First 5 Videos appears in the sidebar under **Create** as soon as they're done, and the Studio stays locked until you've mapped all five.
