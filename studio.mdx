The Studio is where videos get written, which is the part everyone skips and then wonders why filming feels like pulling teeth. You bring an idea (or show up empty-handed), the AI writes with you, and you leave with a script that sounds like you on a good day.

<Note>
The Studio unlocks once you've finished [North Star](/tasks/north-star), [Citizen](/tasks/citizen), and [WorldCode](/tasks/worldcode), and mapped [Your First 5 Videos](/tasks/first-five-videos). Until then it sits grayed out in the sidebar with a lock on it, and hovering or tapping it tells you what's left.
</Note>

## The shelf (why the AI sounds like you)

Across the top of the Studio runs a slim strip called **the shelf**: five chips, one for each bucket of *your room*, the stuff the AI reads before it writes a single word.

| Bucket | What goes in it | How you add to it |
|---|---|---|
| **My Business** | Who you are, what you sell, who it's for | Note, upload, link |
| **My Voice + Content** | Your own reels and voice notes, so drafts come out sounding like you and not like a LinkedIn thought leader having a Big Day | Reel, voice note |
| **Expert Brain** | Hook libraries, frameworks, breakdowns of creators you study | Note, upload, link |
| **Inspiration Content** | Other people's posts worth studying. Steal mechanics, not content | Reel, link |
| **New Video Ideas** | The backlog. Shower thoughts, half-formed bangers, all of it | Note, voice note |

Click a chip and its panel opens with everything in that bucket. There's a search button for when the room gets big, a **Setup** link, and a running item count on the right so you can see how stocked you are at a glance. On a phone the shelf scrolls sideways.

Click anything in a bucket to read it. From there you can **Ask about this**, **Remix this** on inspiration, or **Develop into script** on an idea.

Removing something works two ways. Notes and voice notes are gone for good. Uploads, reels and links just leave the room and stay in your [Library](/documents).

The fuller the room, the better the writing. An empty room gets you generic AI slop. A stocked room gets you a draft that needs one read-out-loud and a couple of tweaks. This is the whole game.

### The two-minute shortcut

Hit **Setup** on the shelf (or **Build my room** if the start screen is nudging you) and **Build your room** runs in about two minutes:

1. **Add your business info**
2. **Sync Instagram**
3. **Import your top videos** into My Voice + Content, as voice samples
4. **Inspiration accounts (optional)**: name creators you admire, and the app watches them on [Radar](/radar) and imports one top reel from each

Each step shows live status, and a step that fails gets a **Retry**. You can always open the Studio anyway.

Two things also happen on their own the first time you open the Studio. Your finished documents land in **My Business**, and three starter playbooks land in **Expert Brain**: *The Hook Playbook*, *Story Structure That Holds Attention*, and *Short-Form Script Craft*. Add your own on top whenever you like. If you already filled Expert Brain yourself, it's left alone, and if you delete the starters, they stay deleted.

## Starting something

The start screen asks one question: **What do you want to make today?**

Type your idea into the box and send it. Pick the angle underneath first:

- **Growth**: made to reach a new audience
- **Authority**: builds trust with buyers
- **Personal**: builds connection with your people
- **Storytelling**: a personal story with a point

A desk opens and the assistant says hello first, with your idea waiting in the message box. Send it when you're ready.

Or start from a format:

- **Reel**: the same four angles, as a guided interview in four steps. A few rounds of answering questions becomes a script you can film today.
  1. **Brain dump**: unload the idea in your own words and get angles back. Voice-note energy, structure not required.
  2. **Framework**: map the idea into a skeleton you can fill in. Tap the direction you like.
  3. **Structure**: a shootable bracketed script in your own wording.
  4. **Voice pass**: the final spoken script in your voice, ready to film.

  Hit **Continue** whenever you're ready for the next step. There's no rush, and you can keep talking at any step.
- **Carousel**: lands on the strongest angle from your room, then writes it slide by slide. **Turn into reel** rewrites it as a spoken script.
- **5 hooks**: five openers grounded in your room, each with a note on why it works. **Use** writes a reel from that hook, and **Save Idea** parks it on your tracker.
- **Develop my ideas**: picks the most promising thing in your New Video Ideas and builds it out. It stays grayed out until your voice or ideas buckets have something in them.

Already have a card on your [Content Tracker](/content-tracker)? **Write in Studio** on the card opens a desk with its details filled in, ready for you to send.

Below all that sits a quiet **Continue** list of your recent sessions. And if you'd rather be shown than told, the **Tour** button at the top right runs a short walkthrough of the whole screen.

## Your voice, literally

Scripts are written in *your* voice, learned from your reel transcripts. Under the composer you'll see **Voice profile active**, or a nudge to set one up if it isn't. Every finished script also shows what it had to work with, like *Voice profile: 12 reels on file · 4 winning patterns*.

Want to see the rules it's following, or set your own? Go to your profile, find **Voice & style rules**, and hit **Generate from my reels**. The app drafts a style charter from your own content, and it's fully editable. Tighten anything it got wrong and every future draft follows your version.

## The desk

Once a session starts, you're at the desk: shelf up top, conversation below. Talk to it like a writers' room. Redirect, push back, "make the hook meaner." It can take it. Prefer to talk? The mic in the message box turns a voice note into text.

A one-line read from your latest [Weekly Review](/weekly-reviews) sits just above the message box, so what's working this week stays in view. Growth reels can also search the web for what's current, and you'll see **Searching the web** while it does.

When a final script arrives, you'll see **Polishing your draft** with a timer for 15 to 30 seconds. That's the cleanup pass: it aligns the wording to how you talk and strips AI-sounding lines (no "it's not X, it's Y", no em dashes, no stock phrases). Then the draft swaps into its finished form as a **Reel script** card:

- **Hook / Script / Caption**: the whole package, not just the middle. Each has its own copy button.
- **Word count and spoken time**: aim for 90 to 140 spoken words, the range for a reel. Way over? **Tighten to ~45s** does it for you.
- **Copy script only**: just the hook and script, ready for a teleprompter. **Copy** takes everything.
- **Save to tracker**: parks it on your [Content Tracker](/content-tracker) so you can schedule it, film it, and eventually see how it did in [Posts](/metrics).

If you just need to knock out this week's three scripts, **Write this week** and **Draft all** on the [Content Tracker](/content-tracker) are still the shortest path. The Studio is for when you want your room open while you write.

## Picking up where you left off

One quirk worth knowing: a Studio session only saves once you actually reply. Clicking around, opening the start screen, changing your mind: none of it clogs your history. Only real conversations make the list.

Once a session exists, three ways back in:

- The **Continue** list on the Studio start screen: your recent sessions, one click to resume.
- The main sidebar's **Recent chats**: Studio sessions carry a little paintbrush marker and go right back into the desk.
- **⌘K**: the command palette. Pages, actions, recent chats. Search the session, hit Enter, you're there.

## On mobile

The whole thing works on your phone. The shelf scrolls sideways, the desk goes single-pane, and brain-dumping by voice while walking is honestly the intended use. Some of your best scripts will be paced out on a sidewalk.
