The Content Tracker is where you actually *make* content, not just talk about making content. It's a kanban board, a calendar, and a writing workspace all stitched into one.

## The board

Five columns, left to right:

1. **Ideas**: raw seeds. Headlines. Half-thoughts. The "what if I posted about" voice memos in card form.
2. **Outline**: you've decided this one's real. Add structure.
3. **Content**: you're writing the actual script/caption. Most of the work happens here.
4. **Scheduled**: written, dated, ready to ship.
5. **Posted**: done. Live. Move on with your life.

Drag cards between columns to update status. Each card has a publish date, a platform (Twitter/LinkedIn/etc.), a type (post, thread, video), and a medium.

## Custom vs. date sort

Two sort modes at the top of the board:

- **Custom**: drag cards to reorder within a column. Order sticks across reloads.
- **Date**: cards sort by publish date automatically. Drag handles are disabled (no fighting against the sort, sorry).

Switch whenever. Sort preference is just visual, your data doesn't move.

## Calendar view

Toggle to the calendar to see everything by publish date. Useful for noticing gaps, accidental clusters, or that one week where you somehow scheduled six posts on Tuesday.

## Generated calendar

When you first pick an archetype, the app generates a draft calendar: roughly a month of empty slots laid out according to your archetype's content mix (growth / authority / personal) and the pillars associated with it. These slots are flagged as **generated** under the hood, so they're easy to tell apart from ideas you wrote yourself.

You're not stuck with them. Edit, delete, replace, whatever. They're a starting point, not a contract.

## Writing flow inside a card

Click a card to open the detail modal. Stages run along the top, each one a step in the writing workflow:

- **Seeds**: paste references, links, quotes, vibes. Stuff that inspired this piece.
- **Expanded thoughts**: flesh out what you actually want to say. Brain dump.
- **Outline**: structure it.
- **Content**: the actual script, caption, or post. The version you'd publish.

You don't have to use every stage. If you can rip a post in one go, just write it in **Content** and ship. The stages are for when you're stuck and need to break the work down into smaller pieces of suffering.

## Asking for a review

In the **Content** stage, once you have a script written, you'll see a **Request review** button. Hit it. The admin team gets notified, takes a look, and leaves feedback as comments below the script.

Their comments show up under the button. Resolve each one with the ✓ when you've addressed it. When you don't want more feedback, the request clears itself.

Change your mind and don't want a review anymore? Click **Request review** again to cancel. It's a toggle.

## Soft delete

Deleted cards go to a trash state (soft delete) rather than being nuked. If you delete the wrong thing, it's recoverable. There's no UI to restore yet, so yell at support and we'll handle it manually.
