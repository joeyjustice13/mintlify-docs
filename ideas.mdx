**Ideas** is the junk drawer for your brain, in the good way. The thought you had in the car, the reel you want to riff on, the line you'll definitely forget by dinner. It lives in the main sidebar, right under Home. Nothing in here has to be good yet. That's the point.

## Capturing an idea

The box at the top of the page takes whatever you've got:

- **A title**: just the gist.
- **Details**: anything worth remembering.
- **Links**: paste as many as you want.
- **Images**: screenshots, references, that one slide you loved.
- **A voice note**: hit the mic and talk.
- **A folder**: optional. You can always sort it later.

Hit **Add idea** and you're done. It should take less time than it took to read this list.

## Voice notes

A recording is enough on its own. No typing, no title. We transcribe it when you stop and name the idea from your first few words. The transcript stays with the idea, so you can skim it later instead of listening to yourself ramble (no judgment, rambling is how the good stuff gets out).

If the transcription fails, the audio still saves. Your thought is safe.

## Folders

You start with four: Fitness, Health, Content, and Personal. Rename them, recolor them, delete them, make them yours. **New folder** adds your own.

**All ideas** shows everything, and **Uncategorized** holds anything without a folder. Deleting a folder never deletes what's inside. Those ideas just move to Uncategorized.

## Sending an idea to the tracker

When an idea's ready to become a real post, hit **Send to Content Tracker** on its card. It lands in the **Idea** column of your [Content Tracker](/content-tracker) with the same title:

- Your typed details and links go into the card's notes.
- Your voice transcript goes into **Develop the idea**, so [Write for me](/content-tracker#write-for-me-the-ghostwriter) reads it as your own spoken thinking.

The idea stays here, tagged **In Content Tracker**, so you can't send it twice by accident.

## Editing and deleting

The pencil edits, the trash deletes. Deleting removes the idea and its attachments for good, so choose accordingly. If you already sent it to the tracker, that card stays put.

## Ideas vs. everything else

- **Ideas**: raw capture. No pressure, no schedule.
- **[Content Tracker](/content-tracker)**: where an idea turns into a planned, scripted, posted thing.
- **New Video Ideas** in the [Studio](/studio): what the AI reads while it writes with you. Your Ideas page doesn't feed it automatically, so when you want to write one up, send it to the tracker and hit **Write in Studio** on the card.
