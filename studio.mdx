The Studio is where videos get written — which is the part everyone skips and then wonders why filming feels like pulling teeth. You bring an idea (or show up empty-handed), the AI writes with you, and you leave with a script that sounds like you on a good day.

## The room (why the AI sounds like you)

The rail on the left is **your room**: five buckets the AI reads before it writes a single word.

- **My Business**: who you are, what you sell, who it's for.
- **My Voice + Content**: your transcripts and posts, so drafts come out sounding like you and not like a LinkedIn thought leader having a Big Day.
- **Expert Brain**: your frameworks, opinions, the stuff you'd say on a podcast.
- **Inspiration Content**: other people's posts worth studying. Steal mechanics, not content.
- **New Video Ideas**: the backlog. Shower thoughts, half-formed bangers, all of it.

The fuller the room, the better the writing. An empty room gets you generic AI slop. A stocked room gets you a draft that needs one read-out-loud and a couple of tweaks. This is the whole game.

Fastest way to fill it: the **Setup** link in the rail. It auto-builds your room from your Instagram in about two minutes. Do that first, thank yourself later.

The rail collapses to an icon strip when you want the screen back. On mobile it's a drawer — open it when you need it, ignore it when you don't.

## Starting something

The start screen asks one question: *What do you want to make today?* Two ways in.

**The composer.** Type what you want to make — "a reel about why most coaches undercharge" — hit Enter, and a desk opens with the request already running. No blank page, no setup ritual. Just go.

**The guided pipelines.** Hit **Reel** and pick a lane: **Growth**, **Authority**, **Personal**, or **Storytelling**. Each is a four-step guided interview:

1. **Brain dump**: ramble. Voice-note energy. Structure not required.
2. **Framework**: the AI proposes the angle and the spine of the video.
3. **Structure**: hook, beats, payoff. You approve or push back.
4. **Voice pass**: the draft gets rewritten in your voice, using what's in your room.

At the end: a shootable script, saved to your [Content Tracker](/content-tracker). Twenty-ish minutes of answering questions becomes a video you can film today.

Also on the start screen: **5 hooks** and **Develop my ideas**, one-click starters for when you have a script that needs openers or a pile of ideas that need shaping. Carousel is sitting there greyed out with a "coming soon" tag. We know. We're on it.

## The desk

Once a session starts, you're at the desk: rail on the left, conversation on the right. Talk to it like a writers' room — redirect, push back, "make the hook meaner." It can take it.

When a script is ready it lands as a **deliverable card**:

- **Hook / Script / Caption**: the whole package, not just the middle.
- **Word-count gauge**: tuned to roughly 90–140 words, the filmable range for a reel. If you're way over, you're writing a podcast. Trim it.
- **"Written in your voice" receipt**: confirmation the draft went through your room's context and a final cleanup pass that strips out AI-sounding lines before the script lands.

**Copy** it straight out, or **Save to tracker** to park it on your board as a card — schedule it, film it, and eventually measure it against your [Metrics](/metrics).

## Picking up where you left off

Sessions don't evaporate. Three ways back in:

- The **Continue** list on the Studio start screen: your recent sessions, one click to resume.
- The main sidebar's **Recent chats**: Studio sessions carry a little paintbrush marker and open right back into the desk.
- **Cmd+K**: the command palette. Search the session, hit Enter, you're there.

## On mobile

The whole thing works on your phone. The rail becomes a drawer, the desk goes single-pane, and brain-dumping by voice while walking is honestly the intended use. Some of your best scripts will be paced out on a sidewalk.
