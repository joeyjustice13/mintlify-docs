This is the manual. Read what's relevant, skip what isn't. The whole point is that you can build your brand without having to remember every little thing.

## What the app actually is

At its core, two things:

- **Tasks**: short, structured interviews where you answer questions and the app builds you a finished document at the end (your positioning, your offer, your story, whatever the task is for). One-and-done. You can come back and read the document any time, and other parts of the app will use it as context automatically.
- **Projects**: open-ended AI chat assistants, each pre-loaded with a specific job (writing posts, brainstorming hooks, planning a launch, etc.). Unlimited conversations. You can start fresh whenever.

Tasks build your foundation. Projects do work on top of it. That's the whole model.
