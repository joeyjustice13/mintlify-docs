The **Course** is the curriculum: lessons, resources, and a chat that can answer questions about them. It's in the main sidebar, right under Ideas. Go at your own pace. Nobody's timing you.

## Browsing

Lessons are grouped by module, in order. The line at the top of the page keeps score (something like *3 of 24 complete*), and you can check a lesson off right from the list. A **Resources** tag means that lesson comes with extras.

## Inside a lesson

Open a lesson and you get:

- **The lesson itself**: usually a video, sometimes written.
- **The description**: what it covers.
- **Resources**: links and downloads that go with it, when there are any.
- **Mark as complete**: click it again if you marked it by accident.
- **Previous** and **Next**: so you don't have to bounce back to the list.

## Chat with a lesson

Hit **Chat with transcript** on a lesson and a chat opens that has the full lesson text loaded. Ask it to explain the part you didn't get, or to apply it to your actual business. On desktop it docks beside the lesson. On a phone it opens as a full-width sheet.

No transcript on that lesson yet? The button reads **Chat about this lesson** instead, and the chat works from the description and tells you so up front. It won't pretend to quote what it hasn't read.

Your thread sticks around for next time. **New conversation** starts fresh.

## Chat with the whole course

**Chat with the course**, at the top of the course page, has every lesson transcript loaded at once and tells you which lesson an answer came from. So when you know you heard it somewhere but can't remember where, ask:

- "Which lesson covered pricing?"
- "What's the difference between the two hook frameworks?"
- "I'm stuck on my offer. What should I rewatch?"

The button only shows up once at least one lesson has a transcript.

## Caveats

- Your account might include the course without the chat. If so, you won't see the chat buttons, and nothing's broken.
- Lessons without a transcript are only known to the course chat by their title.
- The Course and [Recordings](/recordings) are different things. The Course is the curriculum. Recordings are the live coaching calls.
