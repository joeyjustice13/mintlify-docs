The **Posts** tab pulls your Instagram posts into the app so you can see what's actually working without falling into the for-you-page abyss (we've all been there, it's 2am, the algorithm has won). Once a day we hit your handle, grab the latest, and stuff it in here. Public data only. We're not snooping in your DMs.

Quick map of where your numbers live now: your **dashboard** (the home page) shows the insight layer, so followers, your weekly posting ring, engagement, your top post, the follower trend, your posting rhythm, and your current breakout posts all greet you the moment you log in. The **Posts** page is the deep view: the full sortable table of everything we've synced, with Remix one click away. It's not in the sidebar, on purpose. You reach it from your dashboard: click your account chip up top, or the **All posts** link on the Winning now section. Outlier alert notifications land you there too.

## What we pull

For your account, daily-ish:

- Follower count, following count, total post count
- Profile pic, display name, bio
- Your latest ~30 posts: caption, thumbnail, likes, comments, views, post type, posted date

We snapshot follower count once per day so you get a trend line as the days stack up. Other accounts? Don't care, don't store them. Posting on your behalf? Absolutely not, we're not THAT kind of app. We pull stats. That's the whole pitch.

## Setting it up

You added your Instagram handle during onboarding so this should already be quietly running. If it's somehow not, click your name in the sidebar profile menu, open the editor, and add or fix the handle.

The first sync runs when you hit **Sync now** on your dashboard's connect card. Takes ~30 seconds because Apify has to actually go visit your profile. After that, the daily cron picks you up and you don't have to think about it.

## Your dashboard (the glance version)

The home page carries the summary so you never have to go digging for the basics:

- **Pulse strip**: followers with a 30-day delta and mini trend line, a weekly posting ring (3 posts a week is the goal, the ring fills as you post), average engagement over the last 30 days vs. the 30 before, and your top post's multiple vs. your median.
- **Follower trend**: your snapshot line over the last 90 days.
- **Posting rhythm**: a 28-day grid, one cell per day, filled when you posted. Hover a filled cell for that day's post(s). Below it, your **best posting window** (day + 3-hour slot, computed from your own last 90 days, in your own timezone). The window only appears once you have 10+ scored posts, because a "best time" based on two data points is astrology.
- **Winning now**: your top 3 outlier posts from the last 30 days, each with a multiplier chip and a Remix button. There's a second tab for your niche, fed by [Radar](/radar).

The dashboard is the read. The Posts page is where you act on it.

## The Posts page

A profile row up top (avatar, handle, follower/post counts, last-synced dot, plus **Manage** and **Refresh**), then the table. If you've connected a second account, a switcher shows up here too.

### All posts table

Every post we have on you, sortable by:

- **Date** (default, newest first, because that's how you actually think about your content)
- **Likes**
- **Comments**
- **Views**
- **Engagement rate** = (likes + comments) divided by current follower count

Click a column header to sort. Click again to flip direction. It's a table. You know how tables work.

### Filtering by type

Pills above the table: **All · Reels · Image · Carousel**. Click one to narrow. Reels and standalone video posts get grouped together because on modern Instagram those are functionally the same thing (RIP IGTV, you weren't missed).

### Outliers

Posts whose engagement rate is at least 1.5x your median get a **multiplier chip** right in the engagement column, and the chip heats up as the multiple climbs: green from 1.5x, amber from 3x, red from 5x. Same chip, same colors, everywhere it appears (dashboard, Winning now, this table), so a score always reads the same.

This is the entire reason this page exists. Sort by engagement, find the chips, study what those posts have in common. That's your content strategy update for the week, no spreadsheet needed.

Want the AI to do that pattern-finding for you instead? That's what [Weekly Reviews](/weekly-reviews) is for. Same data, automated read, plus pre-built content ideas you can drop into your tracker.

### Remix

Every row in the posts table has a **Remix** button (it glows accent-orange on your outliers, which is a hint). Click it on a post that popped off and the AI does two things:

1. **Breaks down why it worked.** The actual mechanic: the hook structure, the format, the emotional beat, with evidence pulled from your caption and your numbers. Not vibes, receipts.
2. **Generates 3 remix ideas.** Same winning mechanic, fresh angles. Each one respects your archetype's pillars.

Hit **Add** on the ones you like and they drop straight into your [Content Tracker](/content-tracker)'s Ideation column, seeds included. The algorithm just told you what works. This button makes you act on it before the dopamine wears off. Not feeling the three? **More ideas** regenerates a fresh set from the same post.

You can remix any post, not just outliers. Remixing your worst post is a choice, but you're an adult.

### Outlier alerts

When the daily sync spots a fresh outlier (a post from the last 7 days running at 1.5x your median or better), you get a notification in the app: which post, the exact multiple, and a one-tap link straight into **Remix** for that post while the algorithm is still in love with you. Tap it and the breakdown's already loading, no hunting through the table. One alert per post, ever. We celebrate, we don't spam. Your dashboard's "Do this next" card will also nudge you about it, because a post taking off is exactly the moment to act.

## Refresh

The Refresh button kicks off a fresh sync on demand. Takes ~30 seconds because Apify is doing real work. Use it when you just posted and want it reflected, or when something looks suspiciously stale.

## Caveats (the boring but important part)

- **Engagement rate uses your *current* follower count for every post.** So old posts from when you had fewer followers look weaker than they actually were at the time. We'll fix this eventually by snapshotting follower count at post time. For now: weigh recent posts against each other, not against your 2 a.m. throwback from 2023.
- **We only pull ~30 posts per sync.** Deep back catalog? Only the most recent ones show up.
- **Private accounts won't work.** Apify can't scrape what it can't see. Public your account if you want this to do anything.
- **0-follower edge case:** engagement-rate math breaks at zero followers (divide by zero is still illegal in 2026, the courts haven't budged). Sync once, then it works.

Now go find the chips.
