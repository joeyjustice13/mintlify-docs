The Flag is the third foundation task, and it sits between your Citizen and your WorldCode. The North Star is *you*. The Citizen is *who you serve*. The Flag is *where you stand*: the position that makes your world recognizable, so the right people see it and think "oh, that's mine."

It's the difference between "I'm a fitness coach" (so is everyone, babe) and a position nobody else on your audience's feed is holding.

## What it is

A Flag has four layers:

1. **Who**: your ideal client, described as a situation they'd recognize themselves in.
2. **Problem**: the one problem they come to you with, in *their* words, plus what they want instead.
3. **Approach**: what you actually do differently. Not adjectives like "holistic" or "sustainable". A real decision: what you do first, what you hold off on, what you never do.
4. **You**: one thing that shows you understand their situation and one thing that shows you can fix it.

The AI builds all four from evidence you've already given it: your best clients, your Citizen research, your North Star, and what the accounts in your space are already claiming. It doesn't make up a fancy method name to make you look different. If you're different, it finds where. If you're not (yet), it tells you, and leans on the other three layers.

## How it works

### Step 1: Your best clients (only if you need it)

Your Flag is built on what actually happened with real clients, so it needs your best ones. If you did the Citizen recently, you already gave them, and you skip straight to step 2.

If your Citizen was built before that question existed, the Flag asks for them first. Copy the template once per client (three minimum, five if you've got them): who they were, the problem they came with, what they'd tried, what else they were considering, what you did differently, and the result. Short answers are fine, their exact words are even better, and the mic button works here too. Your answers get added to your Citizen doc.

### Step 2: Pick who you're compared to

Add **5 to 10 Instagram accounts** in your space: the ones your audience follows alongside you, the ones you admire, the ones you feel compared to. Accounts already on your [Radar](/radar) show up as one-click fills, and new ones get added to your Radar (unless it's full).

The app reads each account's bio, pinned posts, recent captions, and what they actually say in their recent reels, plus **your own account**, to map what everyone already claims. That takes about **10 to 20 minutes**. Close the tab and go live your life. We'll notify you when it's done.

If an account can't be read (private, deleted, barely posts), you'll be asked to swap it for another one.

<Note>
No competitors you want to name? Hit **Skip this step**. Your Flag gets built from your North Star and Citizen alone. It still works, it just hasn't been checked against what everyone else is saying.
</Note>

### Step 3: Three questions about how you work

This is the only part where you answer questions. Three of them, one at a time:

1. **What do they get wrong?** The AI shows you how most of the accounts you picked approach your clients' problem and asks what you believe instead.
2. **What does that force you to do?** What you do first, what you hold off on, and what you never do that they'd do right away.
3. **What would a client notice?** What feels different in the first two weeks with you, compared to the last thing they tried.

Then it plays your approach back to you as **Belief → Priority → Action** and tells you honestly whether that's a real difference from your space or what everyone else says too. Correct it if it's off.

### Step 4: React to your Flag

The AI shows you what the evidence says (citing your best clients and the accounts you picked), then **one recommended Flag**. If your evidence genuinely points two ways, you might also get up to two alternates, each changing just one layer.

Your job is to react. If something's off, say which part: **who**, **problem**, **approach**, or **you**. It rebuilds that layer and shows you again. Loop until it sounds like you and your best clients would say "that's me."

### Step 5: Confirm it

When you say a clear yes, your Flag is saved word for word. No surprise rewrite after you approved it.

## What you get

A document called your **Flag**, containing:

- **The association**: your position in under 15 words
- **The statement**: all four layers as a couple of sentences you can read out loud
- **Ideal client, Problem, Approach, You**: each layer on its own
- **Why they'd choose you over**: the alternatives your clients would otherwise pick, and where each falls short for them
- **What you don't do**: the tradeoff (this feeds Tourists in your WorldCode)
- **Other problems you'll talk about**: real problems your clients have that orbit the Flag as content
- **What you're against**: the raw material for your enemy, which gets its name in your [WorldCode](/tasks/worldcode)
- **Your proof**, plus **proof to collect next**: the evidence that backs up your Flag, and what to go get from clients if something isn't backed up yet

Saved to your library, added to **My Business** in your [Studio](/studio), and used by the WorldCode task.

## Tips

- **Don't make it broader.** The urge to "not exclude anyone" is strong. The AI will push back, kindly. A narrow Flag you own beats a wide one nobody notices. You can widen a shelf you own, you can't widen one you were never on.
- **Decisions, not adjectives.** "I'm evidence-based" isn't an approach. "I don't touch your diet for the first four weeks" is.
- **Your enemy can wait.** If you start ranting about what you're against, the AI notes it and saves it for your WorldCode. That's where it gets named.
- **No bio line yet.** Ask for one and it'll tell you the line comes from the Flag. Lock the four layers first.
- **Give it real clients.** Your Flag is only as specific as your best-client answers. Vague clients, vague Flag.

## Updating it later

On your Flag document page, **Update competitors** lets you pick a new set of accounts and rebuild. You can do that once a day. Your current Flag stays put until you confirm the new one.

## Already had your WorldCode before the Flag existed?

Then the Flag is **optional** for you. Nothing is locked, your Studio stays exactly where it is, and the Flag shows up tagged **Optional** in your foundation.

If you do build one, your Flag page offers **Rebuild your WorldCode from your Flag**, which lines your enemy, your people, and your language up with your new position. Your current WorldCode stays in place until the new one is done, and **Keep my current WorldCode** backs you out anytime.

## Time

10 to 20 minutes of research you don't have to watch, then about 15 minutes of answering and reacting.

## Prerequisites

You need your **North Star** and your **Citizen** done first. Your WorldCode and First 5 Videos stay locked until the Flag is finished.
