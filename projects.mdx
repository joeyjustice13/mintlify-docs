---
title: "Projects"
description: "Chat assistants that already know who you are."
---

A **project** is an open-ended AI chat assistant with a specific job. Unlike tasks, projects don't end. You can have unlimited conversations, start new sessions whenever, and come back to old ones.

The magic part: projects automatically pull in your task documents as context. So when you open a writing assistant, it already knows your brand voice, your offer, your audience. You don't have to brief it every time.

## Starting a chat

Click any project from the sidebar or **Projects** grid. You'll land on the project's overview page: a list of your past sessions plus a button to start a new one.

Hit the button. The AI opens the conversation. Go.

## Choices

Some projects have **choices**, different "modes" you can pick before starting a session. For example, a content project might have choices for "Twitter post," "LinkedIn post," "Long-form article." Each choice is its own version of the assistant with its own instructions and its own knowledge files.

If a project has choices, you'll see them as separate buttons on the project page. Pick the one that fits what you want to do. The conversation is scoped to that choice.

## Sessions

Every conversation is a **session**. Sessions are saved automatically and titled by the AI after the first reply. You can:

- **Open** an old session and keep going where you left off
- **Rename** a session (right-click in the sidebar)
- **Delete** a session you don't need

There's no limit. Start as many as you want.

## What the AI sees

Each project chat sends the AI:

1. The project's instructions (set by the team)
2. Knowledge files the team uploaded for that project
3. Generated documents from any prerequisite tasks you've completed
4. Documents you explicitly attached via the document picker
5. The conversation history

You can attach extra documents to a specific chat using the document picker in the chat input. Useful when you want the AI to reference something specific without it being part of every conversation forever.

## Prerequisites

Just like tasks, projects can require certain tasks to be done first. If you see a lock screen when you open a project, that's why. Finish the listed prereqs and the project unlocks.

The reason: the project genuinely needs those documents to do good work. Skipping them means worse output.

## Voice + files in chat

Every chat has:

- **Voice input**: hit the mic, talk, it transcribes via Whisper. Useful when you'd rather think out loud.
- **File attach**: drop in an image or document. The AI reads it as part of the current message.
- **Document picker**: paperclip icon. Attach docs from your library to the conversation as persistent context.
