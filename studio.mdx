The Studio is where videos get written — which is the part everyone skips and then wonders why filming feels like pulling teeth. You bring an idea (or show up empty-handed), the AI writes with you, and you leave with a script that sounds like you on a good day.

It unlocks once your three setup interviews are done — [North Star](/tasks/north-star), [Citizen](/tasks/citizen), and [WorldCode](/tasks/worldcode). Until then it sits grayed out in the sidebar, waiting. Finish the interviews and it lights up. That's the only gate.

## The shelf (why the AI sounds like you)

Across the top of the Studio runs a slim strip called **the shelf**: five chips, one for each bucket of *your room* — the stuff the AI reads before it writes a single word.

- **My Business**: who you are, what you sell, who it's for.
- **My Voice + Content**: your reel transcripts and posts, so drafts come out sounding like you and not like a LinkedIn thought leader having a Big Day.
- **Expert Brain**: your frameworks, opinions, the stuff you'd say on a podcast.
- **Inspiration Content**: other people's posts worth studying. Steal mechanics, not content.
- **New Video Ideas**: the backlog. Shower thoughts, half-formed bangers, all of it.

Click a chip and its panel opens: everything in that bucket, with buttons to add a note, a reel, a link, or an upload — and to delete whatever stops earning its spot. There's a search button for when the room gets big, a **Setup** link, and a running item count on the right so you can see how stocked you are at a glance. On mobile the shelf scrolls sideways.

The fuller the room, the better the writing. An empty room gets you generic AI slop. A stocked room gets you a draft that needs one read-out-loud and a couple of tweaks. This is the whole game.

### The two-minute shortcut

Hit **Setup** on the shelf and the app auto-builds your room from your Instagram in about two minutes: business docs, plus your top reels pulled in as voice samples. Do that first, thank yourself later.

If your **Expert Brain** is empty, Setup also seeds three starter playbooks into it: *The Hook Playbook*, *Story Structure That Holds Attention*, and *Short-Form Script Craft*. Add your own on top whenever you like. And if you've already filled Expert Brain yourself, Setup leaves it alone — your homework is never touched.

## Starting something

The start screen greets you in a big serif and asks one question through **the composer**: *"Describe the video you want to make…"* Type it, hit Enter, and a desk opens with your request already running. No blank page, no setup ritual. Just go.

Under it, a row of pills:

- **Reel**: a menu of four guided pipelines — **Growth**, **Authority**, **Personal**, **Storytelling**. Each is a four-step guided interview:
  1. **Brain dump**: ramble. Voice-note energy. Structure not required.
  2. **Framework**: the AI proposes the angle and the spine of the video.
  3. **Structure**: hook, beats, payoff. You approve or push back.
  4. **Voice pass**: the draft gets rewritten in your voice, using what's in your room.

  Twenty-ish minutes of answering questions becomes a shootable script you can film today.
- **Carousel**: sitting there disabled with a "coming soon" tag. We know. We're on it.
- **5 hooks** and **Develop my ideas**: open-ended starters for when you need openers, or a pile of ideas that need shaping.

Below the pills, a quiet **Continue** list of your recent sessions. And if you'd rather be shown than told: the **Tour** pill, top right, runs a short in-app walkthrough of the whole screen.

## Your voice, literally

Scripts are written in *your* voice, learned from your reel transcripts. That's not a metaphor — the deliverable receipt tells you exactly how many of your reels it matched against.

Want to see the rules it's following, or set your own? Go to **Profile → Voice & style rules** and hit **Generate from my reels**. The app drafts a style charter from your own content, and it's fully editable. Tighten anything it got wrong and every future draft follows your version.

## The desk

Once a session starts, you're at the desk: shelf up top, conversation below. Talk to it like a writers' room — redirect, push back, "make the hook meaner." It can take it.

When a script is ready it lands as a **deliverable card**:

- **Hook / Script / Caption**: the whole package, not just the middle.
- **Filmable gauge**: tuned to roughly 90–140 spoken words, the range for a reel. If you're way over, you're writing a podcast. Trim it.
- **"Written in your voice · matched to N reels"**: the receipt that the draft came out of your room, not the void.

Before any card lands, drafts clean themselves up and get a voice check — AI-sounding lines get stripped, wording gets aligned to how you actually talk. You only ever see the version that passed.

**Copy** it straight out, or **Save to tracker** to park it on your board as a card — schedule it, film it, and eventually measure it against your [Metrics](/metrics).

## Picking up where you left off

One quirk worth knowing: a Studio session only saves once you actually reply. Clicking around, opening the start screen, changing your mind — none of it clogs your history. Only real conversations make the list.

Once a session exists, three ways back in:

- The **Continue** list on the Studio start screen: your recent sessions, one click to resume.
- The main sidebar's **Recent chats**: Studio sessions carry a little paintbrush marker and deep-link right back into the desk.
- **⌘K**: the command palette. Pages, actions, recent chats — search the session, hit Enter, you're there.

## On mobile

The whole thing works on your phone. The shelf scrolls sideways, the desk goes single-pane, and brain-dumping by voice while walking is honestly the intended use. Some of your best scripts will be paced out on a sidewalk.
