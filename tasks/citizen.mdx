The Citizen is the second foundation task. The North Star is about *you*. The Citizen is about *the person you're trying to reach*. Both documents power everything downstream.

## What it is

A two-phase task. First, a short intake about your offer and, most importantly, your **best clients**. Then the AI runs real research, pulling from online communities, forums, reviews, and published sources, and builds a deep psychographic profile of your ideal client around what your real clients told you.

The output isn't a generic "marketing persona" with stock-photo Sarah-the-45-year-old-mom energy. It's grounded in what actual people in your niche are actually saying, in the words they actually use, starting with the people you've already helped.

## How it works

### Phase 1: Intake (4 questions)

The AI asks one question at a time:

1. **What do you sell?** Fill in the blanks: `I help [who] to [outcome] through [what you offer].`
2. **Your best clients.** This is the big one. Best means two things: they got real results, and you'd take five more exactly like them. You get a little template to copy once per client (three minimum, five if you have them):
   - **Who**: the type of person and their situation
   - **Problem**: what they wanted fixed when they reached out, in their words if you remember them
   - **Past attempts** (optional): what they'd already tried and why it didn't work
   - **Considering instead** (optional): what else they were weighing, and why they picked you
   - **Your approach**: what you did differently from what they'd tried
   - **Result**: what they got and what they said about it. Paste the DM or testimonial if you have one.
3. **What do they want most** on the other side of working with you? Skip if unsure.
4. **Who's the wrong fit** for your offer? Skip if unsure.

Fewer than three clients so far? Say so, and the AI swaps question 2 for a few questions about who you're trying to attract instead. No shame, everyone starts somewhere.

Before research starts, the AI plays back what it sees across your best clients: who your results cluster around, the problem they came with, and what they'd tried. If that's different from who your offer *says* it's for, it asks which one you want researched. **Don't skip this confirmation step.** If the summary is off, fix it now.

### Phase 2: Research

Once confirmed, research kicks off. This takes a few minutes. You'll see chunks streaming in. Feel free to step away and come back; it'll be done when you get back.

The AI flags anything lower-confidence with `[verify]` so you can sanity-check it independently. If your audience is too niche for reliable data, it'll say so and narrow to the closest adjacent audience.

## What you get

A document called your **Citizen Doc** containing:

- A journal entry written as your ideal client, so you can feel who you're talking to
- Specific observable problems and pain points
- Goals and identity-level motivations
- Fears, obstacles, objections
- Past attempts and why they failed
- The exact language and lingo your audience uses
- Emotional drivers and the dominant internal narrative
- **Your best clients**, word for word, exactly as you wrote them

Anything that came straight from your own clients is tagged **(from your clients)** and outranks the online research everywhere it's used.

Saved to your library, added to **My Business** in your [Studio](/studio), and used by your [Flag](/tasks/flag) and WorldCode. Your best clients are the main evidence your Flag gets built on.

## Tips

- **Spend your time on the best clients.** Real names in your head, real words they said, real results. That one question powers this doc *and* your Flag. Vague clients = vague everything.
- **Use the right kind of detail.** "Online business owners" is too broad. "Service-based coaches doing $5 to $25k/month who haven't built a content engine yet" is workable.
- **Don't try to research a fantasy audience.** Pick the people you've actually talked to, sold to, or seen in your space. The made-up dream client is not coming. Research the real one.

## Time

15 to 30 minutes of active answering, plus a few minutes of background research where you can be doing literally anything else.

## Prerequisites

None. You can do this before, after, or alongside the North Star.
