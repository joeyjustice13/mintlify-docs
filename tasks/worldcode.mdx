The WorldCode is the fourth foundation task and the one that ties it all together. It takes your North Star (who you are), your Citizen (who you serve), and your [Flag](/tasks/flag) (where you stand) and builds the actual architecture of your brand on top of them.

This is the doc that turns "I have a brand" into "I have a *world*": a singular conviction, an enemy, the people who belong inside it, and the language that holds it together. It's the difference between a personal brand and a movement people self-identify into.

## What it is

A guided session where the AI walks you through 7 components in a strict order. Each one builds on the last. You can't skip ahead, and you shouldn't want to. The order is the whole point.

The AI on this one is *not* gentle. It's designed to push back when answers are soft, refuse to move on when a component isn't strong enough, and challenge you to find the sharper version of what you actually believe. This is by design. The output is only valuable if the components are real. This is the task that makes you cry a little, in a good way.

## The 7 components

Built in this exact sequence:

1. **Core Conviction**: the one belief your world is organised around, the thing you'd still say if you had nothing to sell. It starts from the belief in your Flag and digs underneath it. Has to polarise, compel, and be clear enough to spread mouth-to-mouth. The AI will reject candidates that are just "nice"; it's looking for the one that costs you something to say out loud.
2. **Origin Story (anchor)**: the lived experience that earned you the right to your conviction. Not a polished narrative here, just enough to anchor it to something real.
3. **Citizens**: the people who belong inside your world, defined from the brand's perspective.
4. **Tourists**: who this is explicitly *not* for. Just as important as citizens.
5. **Enemy**: the force working against your people (a mindset, a practice, an industry norm, or the type of people who profit from it, never a named person or competitor). You pick it from a few candidates, then choose its name from a ranked list of ten, plus **the trap**: a name for where the enemy leaves your people stuck.
6. **Shared Language**: the name your people call themselves, a name for what you're *for*, the lines you and your people say, how your world looks and sounds on camera, and your **signature sign-off**, the line you end every video on.
7. **The World**: the long-term universe you're building and what it stands for.

## How it differs from the other tasks

- **It's more confrontational.** The AI is built to push back, refuse soft answers, and demand specificity. Don't take it personally; it's pulling out the brand that already exists in you.
- **You can't run it without the other three.** This task literally cannot start without your North Star, Citizen, and Flag.
- **It won't contradict your Flag.** Same ideal client, and your enemy has to contain what your Flag says you're against. If the AI thinks your Flag itself is off, it'll tell you to go back and fix the Flag instead of quietly building around it.
- **The output is a finished doc.** Not a brain dump like the North Star. This is the structured architecture, organised, named, and ready to use.

## What's in your context

The AI gets these automatically:

1. Your **North Star** (source material for Core Conviction, Origin Story, and how your world sounds)
2. Your **Citizen Doc**, including your best clients (source material for Citizens, Tourists, and the audience side of every component)
3. Your **Flag**, plus the read on the accounts you picked if you didn't skip that step (your leading Core Conviction, your enemy's raw material, and the boundary for Citizens and Tourists)
4. The **WorldBranding framework** and **Cult Principles** (the philosophy the AI works from, and how it tests whether each component is strong enough)
5. Two guides for **naming your enemy** and **writing your sign-off**

You don't need to know these reference docs exist for the task to work; the AI handles all of that internally.

## Tips

- **Trust the pushback.** When the AI rejects an answer, it's not being precious. The whole task is engineered to refuse softness.
- **React, don't pick.** When it presents a candidate Core Conviction, your job is to react honestly: agree, push back, redirect. Don't try to pick the "right" answer.
- **It's normal for this to feel intense.** The Core Conviction component especially can take real time. That's expected.
- **Pick names you'd actually say on camera.** For your enemy, your trap, and your sign-off, that's the only test that matters. If none land, ask to see more.
- **No bio lines or hashtags here.** The WorldCode is the foundation. Those get built later, from it.

## What you get

A document called your **WorldCode**, the brand architecture. Saved to your library, added to **My Business** in your [Studio](/studio). This is the doc that lets the AI actually sound like *your* brand instead of a generic creator.

## Time

45 to 90 minutes, depending on how fast you reach a Core Conviction the AI accepts. Component 1 is the longest by far.

## Prerequisites

You must complete the **North Star**, the **Citizen**, and the **Flag** first. The WorldCode page in the app stays locked until all three are done. There is no skipping the line on this one. The lock is the law.

## Already have a WorldCode from before the Flag?

You're good, nothing breaks. If you build your [Flag](/tasks/flag), you can then **Rebuild your WorldCode from your Flag** so your enemy, your people, and your language line up with your new position. Your current WorldCode stays in place until the new one is done, and **Keep my current WorldCode** at the top of the page backs you out anytime.
