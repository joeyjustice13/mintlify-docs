Okay so here's the move. On your first login, three things happen, in this order:

## 1. Welcome video

A short Vimeo walkthrough auto-plays the first time you sign in. Watch it (or don't, it's saved if you want it later under **Profile → Introduction Video**).

## 2. Pick your archetype

After the video, you'll be asked to choose an **archetype**: Explorer, Sovereign, or Cartographer. This isn't a vibes quiz. It tells the app what mix of content you want to make (growth content vs. authority content vs. personal content) and which content pillars to lean on.

You can change this any time under **Profile → Select Archetype**. Changing it doesn't nuke anything you've already made; it just changes what the app suggests going forward.

## 3. Look at your task list

The home page shows your tasks. They run in a specific order, and some are **locked** until you finish earlier ones. That's on purpose. Each task builds on the last.

Hit the first unlocked task. Answer the questions. When you're done, the app generates a document and you move on.

## How long this takes

The first task takes ~10 to 20 minutes if you actually think about your answers. Don't rush. The whole point of the tasks is that they pry good answers out of you, the kind you wouldn't have written in a blank doc.

If you have to stop partway through, you can. Your progress saves automatically. Just come back later and pick up where you left off.

## What you'll have when you're done

After working through the tasks:

- A document library full of your own positioning, brand story, voice, offers, etc.
- Project chats (writing assistants, strategists, whatever) that already know all of that about you.
- A content tracker pre-loaded with a calendar that matches your archetype.

That's it. That's the on-ramp.
